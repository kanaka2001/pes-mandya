## 🏢 Before the Cloud: Traditional On-Premises IT

- Organizations bought, housed, and maintained their own servers
- Capacity had to be guessed in advance, leading to over-provisioning or shortages
- Procurement took weeks or months
- Large up-front capital expenditure (CapEx)
- The organization handled power, cooling, hardware failure, and security

---

## ☁️ After the Cloud

- Rent computing resources on demand over the internet
- Pay only for what you use (operating expenditure, OpEx)
- Provision resources in minutes
- The provider manages the physical data centers

---

## ⚖️ Comparison

| Aspect | On-premises | Cloud |
|---|---|---|
| Cost model | High up-front CapEx | Pay-as-you-go OpEx |
| Time to launch | Weeks to months | Minutes |
| Scaling | Buy more hardware | Scale up or down on demand |
| Maintenance | Your team | Provider (for the infrastructure) |
| Capacity planning | Guess in advance | Adjust as needed |

---

## 🚀 Business Drivers

- **Agility:** try ideas quickly and shorten time to market
- **Scalability:** grow or shrink with demand
- **Cost efficiency:** no idle hardware
- **Global reach:** deploy close to users worldwide
- **Focus:** spend effort on the product, not on data centers

> 💡 The cloud turns infrastructure into a service you consume on demand.
