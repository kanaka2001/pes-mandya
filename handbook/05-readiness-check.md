## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| Cannot sign in | Re-check account ID or alias, username, and password. Ask a facilitator. |
| Slow or no Wi-Fi | Reconnect, or use a mobile hotspot. |
| Services not visible | Check you are in the correct Region. |
| Access denied | Report it to a facilitator. |
