# Before You Begin

**What you need**

- A laptop with a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge)
- Stable Wi-Fi (verified in Session 4)
- An AWS account or the login details provided by the facilitators
- A phone or authenticator app if your account uses MFA

**Ground rules**

- Use only the Region your facilitator names.
- Never share your passwords or access keys.
- Delete or stop the resources you create once a lab ends, to avoid charges.
- Ask questions at any time.
